Alpine Resort Dome Project - Austria

Factory-direct transparent dome houses delivered to a mountain resort - modular installation, German PC panels and aviation-grade aluminum frames.

Project Overview

A luxury mountain resort in Austria selected Domemill as its factory-direct supplier for 24 transparent dome suites. The client needed weather-proof accommodation that could survive alpine winters, blend with the landscape and open within one season.

Our dedicated engineering team deepened the structural drawings for heavy-snow loading, and the factory produced every dome with German-imported PC panels and aviation-grade aluminum frames. Modular bolt-assembly meant the resort's own crew installed each dome in just 2-3 days - no heavy machinery, no concrete foundation, zero damage to the natural ground.

After the first season, occupancy of the dome suites reached 95%, and the resort has since placed two repeat orders.
