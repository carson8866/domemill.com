Luxury Glamping Dome with Ensuite Bathroom Option

The Luxury Glamping Dome is our most popular model for upscale camping resorts and boutique hotels. Sized from 5m to 9m, it combines German-imported PC double-wall panels with an aviation-grade aluminum frame for year-round comfort in any climate.

Every dome is modular and pre-drilled, so your crew can complete installation in 1-3 days without heavy machinery. Add an ensuite bathroom, blackout curtains, hot tub or deck - we adapt the layout to your site and market.

  • German PC panels with 10-year UV guarantee
  • Aviation-grade aluminum frame - wind & snow rated
  • Modular assembly - 2 workers, 1-3 days
  • Low MOQ from 1 set, OEM/ODM welcome

German Imported PC Materials

Every dome is built with double-wall polycarbonate imported from Germany - UV protected, impact resistant and crystal clear. Frames are precision-cut from aviation-grade 6061 aluminum for strength without the weight.
