Who We Are

Factory Direct Manufacturer of Transparent Dome Houses

Baoding Domemill Manufacturing Co., Ltd. is an ISO-certified manufacturer located in Baoding, Hebei Province, China. Since our founding, we have specialized in the research, design and production of transparent dome houses, glamping domes and polycarbonate structures for the hospitality industry.

With a state-of-the-art manufacturing facility, fully automated production lines and a dedicated engineering team, we deliver factory-direct to resorts, campgrounds, hotels, developers and professional buyers in more than 30 countries.
