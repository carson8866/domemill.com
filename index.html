F.A.Q

Frequently Asked Questions by Global Buyers

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

For standard models the MOQ is just 1 set - perfect for testing the market. For fully customized OEM/ODM orders the MOQ is 5 sets. Large resort and hotel projects are always welcome, whatever the quantity.

How long is production and delivery?

Standard domes ship in 15-25 days. Custom sizes take 25-45 days depending on design complexity. We arrange safe container shipping to any port worldwide, with complete export documentation and insurance available.

Do you provide installation support?

Yes. Every dome is modular with numbered components, a detailed manual and assembly video, so 2 workers can build a standard dome in 1-3 days. Engineer on-site guidance is available for large or complex projects.

What certifications do you hold?

We are ISO 9001 certified and CE compliant. German PC panels and aviation-grade aluminum frames can pass SGS or BV third-party inspection, and full test reports are provided with each shipment.

Can you make custom sizes and designs?

Absolutely. Our dedicated engineering team provides free drawing deepening for custom sizes, layouts and structures. OEM/ODM services also include your branding, color scheme and custom packaging.

What is the warranty and after-sales service?

We provide a 5-year structural warranty on aluminum frames and a 10-year UV guarantee on German PC panels. Our export team offers lifetime technical support and fast spare-parts delivery.
